# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no build step, no npm. Deployed as-is on GitHub Pages (Edupizzol/Edupizzol.github.io). At most one web font family, always with a system fallback.

## Users

Primary: technical evaluators and recruiters screening AI internship candidates, specifically iFood (genAI, AI Engineer profile) and Serasa Experian (Data track). They skim on a laptop between other applications. The iFood evaluator stated what they look for: ML implementations on GitHub, published models, research projects, competitions and technical articles. Secondary: software engineering reviewers who still value the SWE projects.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Eduardo Jesus Dal Pizzol, Software Engineering student (4th semester, UnB FCTE, Brasília). Success: an evaluator finds the ML/AI evidence (competitions, papers with code, the MCTI LLM work) within seconds, trusts the numbers, and reaches the CV or contact.

## Positioning

Builds AI systems end to end and states their limits: every result carries its caveat ("em andamento", "do time", "calibração de desenvolvimento", "indício para o servidor"). Honesty about what does not work yet is part of the pitch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Portuguese only (pt-BR).
- index.html with short cards + one detail page per main project in projetos/ (mcti, pmi-krilltech, civicwater-sentinel, artigos).
- Codeforces rating fetched client-side from the public API; on failure, show only the profile link.
- Content is fixed by the owner: no invented metrics, results, technologies or dates; caveats must stay.
- MCTI work is internal: no screenshots, company names, CPFs or values beyond the approved text. Code is not public.
- Links still pending as placeholders are omitted until the owner supplies them.

## Brand Commitments

None carried over: the previous look (graphite, amber, Space Grotesk + Newsreader) is explicitly retired. Owner rejects: template/startup look, fake terminal/hacker styling, heavy animation, dense cards.

## Evidence on Hand

- curriculo.pdf (latest CV).
- Public links: GitHub, LinkedIn, Codeforces, PMI repo, CivicWater public dashboard, two paper PDFs and code repo in Edupizzol/Papers and Edupizzol/odonto-cnn, Parnas deploy, Juiz Online deploy.
- No photo, no project screenshots, no logos. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence first: the AI/ML proof is findable in one scroll.
2. Every number is sourced and keeps its caveat.
3. Scannable over exhaustive: cards stay short; detail lives on project pages.
4. Sober and technical; craft shows in precision, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes (follows prefers-color-scheme). Keyboard navigable. Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Works 360–1920 px without horizontal scroll.
